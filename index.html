How YouTube access works

The Google OAuth connection is for the authorized operator of the Obby Chaos channel. When enabled, the app requests only the youtube.upload permission needed to upload videos. It is not a public sign-in or viewer account service.

Public availability of a source video does not establish permission to republish it. Publication must pass separate rights checks. YouTube, Google, and Roblox do not sponsor or endorse this tool.
