Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026

Obby Chaos AutoPilot ("the App") is a private, creator-operated video production and YouTube publishing automation project. This policy describes the intended YouTube integration as it is being developed. The App is not offered as a general-purpose public login service.

1. Who operates the App

The App is operated for the Obby Chaos YouTube channel by the KOSTdeveloper project operator. For inquiries or requests, contact the operator via their GitHub profile.

2. Google data and permissions

When the channel operator explicitly connects a Google account, the planned integration requests the minimum permission needed to publish videos: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. Google displays the requested permission at authorization. The App does not need the operator's Google password.

The App may handle OAuth credentials (including a refresh token and short-lived access tokens), the uploaded video's file and metadata (such as title, description, and visibility), and the response returned by the YouTube API (including the uploaded video ID and status). It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, or contacts.

3. How information is used

  • To authenticate the authorized channel operator with Google OAuth.
  • To upload operator-approved video files and metadata to the operator's YouTube channel.
  • To confirm uploads, maintain publication records, prevent accidental duplicate uploads, and diagnose failures.
  • To enforce independent checks that the content is permitted to be published.

Information received through Google APIs is used for these purposes, not to sell personal data or to train general-purpose AI models. Production services such as video analysis or voice generation are separate from the Google account authorization data.

4. Where data is stored and who receives it

OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are intended to be stored as restricted GitHub Actions repository secrets rather than committed to public source code. Short-lived access tokens are used only for authorized API calls. Operational records may store uploaded video IDs, source references, timestamps, checksums, and upload results. Video and metadata submitted for publication are transmitted to Google/YouTube to provide the requested service.

GitHub hosts this informational site and may process standard access logs under its own policies. No advertising or visitor analytics scripts are intentionally included in this site. Service providers may process limited operational data as needed to provide their services. The operator does not intentionally send Google OAuth tokens to third-party video-generation or analysis tools.

5. Retention and security

Operational data is retained only as needed to maintain publication integrity, troubleshoot errors, comply with relevant requirements, and avoid duplicate publications. The operator may remove it upon request where feasible. Technical safeguards are intended to include restricted secrets, least-privilege OAuth scopes, access controls, and avoidance of credential logging. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices, revocation, and deletion

The connected Google account owner can review or revoke authorization at any time in Google Account third-party connections. Revoking authorization prevents new uploads using that grant, subject to Google processing. To request deletion of records held by the App operator, contact KOSTdeveloper. Removing local records will not automatically delete videos already published on YouTube; those can be managed using YouTube Studio.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the App's features or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected on this page, with an updated effective date.

This site describes an owner-operated workflow under development. It does not grant access to any user's YouTube account without their explicit Google OAuth consent.
